# Original Cover Prompt Examples

Use these as **concept seeds**, not fixed layouts. Follow `../reference/cover-art-style.md` for the house style, safety checks and full prompt rules. Each example is a conceptual illustration, not a screenshot or documentary record of a production system.

## 1. AI-friendly codebase — The workshop catalog

**Thesis:** an agent can navigate an environment more reliably when tools, tests and paths are discoverable.

**Metaphor:** a meticulous instrument maker organizes labeled-but-unreadable tool shapes and fitted drawers, while several mechanical helpers can locate a specific tool through a central catalog mechanism. Avoid readable labels and letters.

**Prompt seed:** Original monochrome archival copperplate engraving, wide landscape. Inside a precise old scientific instrument workshop, a central mechanical catalog routes small clockwork assistants to dedicated drawers of tools; orderly channels and calibration instruments connect the stations. Show structure enabling reliable work, not an anthropomorphic robot coding. Fine cross-hatching, black/ivory palette, textured paper, one focal mechanism, clear silhouette at thumbnail size, no lettering, digits, logos, modern screens or photorealism.

## 2. Guarded orchestration — Isolated foundry chambers

**Thesis:** independent workspaces and controlled promotion limit the impact of unsafe automation.

**Metaphor:** multiple sealed foundry chambers execute work separately, but each workpiece passes through a guarded inspection hatch before joining the central assembly.

**Prompt seed:** Wide black-and-ivory scientific etching of a massive historical foundry cutaway. Separate workshops with thick partitions each contain a distinct mechanical task; a guarded narrow gate inspects pieces before entry into a clean central corridor. Emphasize containment boundaries and review, not a realistic depiction of Docker or Git. Meticulous copper engraving, cross-hatching, deliberate perspective, subtle archival-paper texture, no labels, lettering, numbers, logos, modern UI or neon.

## 3. LIMIT/OFFSET pagination — The indexed archive

**Thesis:** a direct navigation mechanism can avoid repeatedly traversing already-known preceding entries, subject to documented query and index constraints.

**Metaphor:** a vast archive of shelving recedes into the distance, and an archivist uses a precise mechanical index/rail shortcut rather than inspecting every shelf along a long route.

**Prompt seed:** Editorial monochrome engraved plate of an immense historical archive seen in perspective. One sharply rendered mechanical indexing rail connects a cataloging desk to a distant marked location among hundreds of orderly shelves; a longer looping walkway contrasts with the direct route. Show route complexity without unsupported benchmark data. Dense but elegant engraved ink lines, archival-gray paper, one clear focal pointer, landscape, no legible text, digits, logos, code, or modern monitors.

## 4. CI/CD reliability — The inspected assembly line

**Thesis:** delivery speed is only useful when verification, release gates and rollback paths are designed together.

**Metaphor:** an antique mechanical assembly line produces precision artifacts through distinct stations, with a test instrument, diversion gate, and a reversible alternate path.

**Prompt seed:** Wide copperplate engineering etching showing a coherent historic precision manufacturing line, one workpiece moving through build, inspection and guarded dispatch mechanisms, with a subtle visible bypass rail for safe return. Make the inspection gate the primary focal point. Monochrome charcoal ink, fine hatch marks, strong spatial logic and thumbnail readability, worn scientific-paper texture; no titles, diagrams with readable labels, digital interfaces, fake graphs, logos, neon or glossy 3D.

## 5. Reliability and capacity — The pressure-regulated canal

**Thesis:** when a shared resource saturates, independent flow channels and pressure relief can limit cascading overload.

**Metaphor:** a series of historical aqueduct channels with regulated sluice gates, one overflowing channel and well-protected neighboring channels.

**Prompt seed:** Detailed wide monochrome architectural engraving of a historical waterworks station. Separate canals share a reservoir but use solid partitions and mechanical spillways; the main scene focuses on a carefully calibrated sluice relieving pressure while other channels remain stable. Clear systems metaphor; avoid implying real production telemetry. Fine cross-hatched engraving, charcoal on off-white, rich workshop detail and coherent perspective, safe crop margins, no textual labels, numbers, logos, or colored CGI.

## Variation policy

Before using an example, compare the last three published covers when available. If the subject, scene, perspective or dominant object repeats, change at least two major choices; do not merely substitute the article title. Avoid using all five examples with an identical central desk, robed person or color grading.
