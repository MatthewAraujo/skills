# AkitaOnRails — optional historical editorial notes

**Not a default style guide and not a template.** Read only when the user explicitly requests a study of Akita's editorial approach. For ordinary writing, prioritize `../SKILL.md`, `narrative-modes.md` and `editorial-rubric.md`.

## Transferable techniques

- Begin with a specific engineering claim, discovery, or question.
- Ground opinions in implementation evidence and real trade-offs.
- Be direct about constraints, failed assumptions and limits when observed.
- Explain mechanisms thoroughly without sacrificing relevance to the reader.
- Close with a useful, bounded conclusion instead of an obligatory moral.

## Boundaries

Do not copy Fabio Akita's writing voice, humor, catchphrases, argumentative habits or recognizable section order. In particular, do not force every article into "Problem → What I built → Architecture → Numbers → Lessons → Conclusion." Explore other valid forms in `narrative-modes.md`.
