# visual-ui-polisher

Use this skill to improve the visual quality of an existing application screen.

This skill focuses on making the interface look more polished, modern, balanced, and professional without changing the product scope or business logic.

Review the screen using the following criteria:

1. Visual hierarchy
- Is the most important information immediately visible?
- Are titles, subtitles, actions, and data visually prioritized?
- Are primary actions visually stronger than secondary actions?

2. Layout composition
- Is the screen balanced?
- Is there too much empty space or too much density?
- Are sections grouped clearly?
- Does the layout guide the eye naturally?

3. Spacing and alignment
- Are margins and paddings consistent?
- Are elements aligned to a clear grid?
- Is spacing used to separate sections logically?

4. Typography
- Are font sizes, weights, and line heights consistent?
- Is the text easy to scan?
- Are labels, headings, metadata, and descriptions visually distinct?

5. Color usage
- Are colors intentional rather than decorative?
- Is the primary color used consistently?
- Are warning, success, error, and neutral states visually clear?
- Are there colors that make the UI look noisy or amateur?

6. Component polish
- Do cards, buttons, inputs, tables, badges, modals, and tabs look consistent?
- Are borders, shadows, radius, and backgrounds coherent?
- Are interactive states visually clear?

7. Visual density
- Is the interface too cramped?
- Is it too sparse?
- Could information be grouped better into cards, sections, panels, or rows?

8. Modern SaaS quality
- Does the screen look like a production-grade SaaS/admin product?
- What makes it feel unfinished?
- What would make it feel more premium?

Output format:

## Overall visual diagnosis
Give a direct assessment of the screen's current visual quality.

## Biggest visual problems
List the main issues hurting the perceived quality of the screen.

## Concrete visual improvements
Suggest specific improvements for layout, spacing, typography, colors, and components.

## Design system recommendations
Suggest reusable tokens, component variants, or theme adjustments.

## Implementation-ready tasks
Convert the recommendations into small frontend tasks.
